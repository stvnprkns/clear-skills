The standard

Understanding is the output.

  1. 01Start with the reader’s question.

    Choose the representation for the decision, not for novelty.

  2. 02Protect the meaning.

    Preserve facts, uncertainty, scale, and comparison integrity.

  3. 03Remove cognitive errands.

    Put the answer where the reader is already looking.
