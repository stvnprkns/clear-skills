# Case: appropriate narrow line domain

**Family:** Restraint

## Prompt

Audit a line chart of reactor temperature deviations from a 100°C target. The safe operating band is 98–102°C, the y-axis spans exactly that range, the band and target are labeled, and the title says “Temperature remained within tolerance.” Hourly values range from 98.7–101.4°C. Operators need to notice small deviations quickly.

## Expected skill behavior

- Keep the disclosed narrow domain because position/change within tolerance is the operational task.
- Do not apply the zero-baseline rule for bars to a line chart.
- Preserve the labeled target and safe band.
- Avoid redesign absent a concrete runtime or accessibility failure.
