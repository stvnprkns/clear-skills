# Case: overplotted time series

**Family:** Create

## Prompt

I need to show weekly conversion rate for eight regions over two years. Leadership wants to understand whether regions have similar seasonal patterns and which regions run consistently higher or lower. What design would you choose?

## Expected skill behavior

- Consider aligned small multiples with shared y-scale because both shape and level matter.
- Preserve common temporal axis and consistent region order.
- Avoid eight equally saturated overlaid lines unless interaction/selection clearly improves the task.
