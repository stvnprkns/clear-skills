# Case: strong simple bar chart

**Family:** Restraint

## Prompt

Audit this chart: six product categories shown as sorted horizontal bars from largest to smallest. All bars are light gray except the category discussed in the accompanying paragraph, which is blue. Values are directly labeled at bar ends. The axis starts at zero and only has two faint reference ticks. The title says “Returns are concentrated in accessories.” Accessories is 34% of returns; the next category is 17%.

## Expected skill behavior

- Recognize that the representation, sorting, zero baseline, labeling, and emphasis hierarchy are all strong.
- Avoid manufacturing low-value redesigns.
- Possibly recommend only a source/denominator/context note if absent and decision-relevant.
- A `Clear` or light `Refine` verdict should be possible.
