# Case: partial period

**Family:** Audit

## Prompt

A dashboard line chart shows monthly support tickets. August is down 62% versus July and is highlighted green as an improvement. Today is August 12. All prior months are complete. What should change?

## Expected skill behavior

- Catch incomplete-period comparability before styling.
- Recommend marking August partial and comparing like-for-like elapsed days or withholding the improvement judgment until the period is comparable.
- Do not treat the apparent drop as a real performance improvement.
