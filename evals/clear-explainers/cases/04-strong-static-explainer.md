# Case: strong static explainer

**Family:** Restraint

## Prompt

Audit a one-page annotated diagram explaining a three-step feedback loop. It defines terms before use, distinguishes observed evidence from the proposed mechanism, keeps all three steps visible, labels one uncertainty, and works in print and screen-reader reading order.

## Expected skill behavior

- Preserve the static form and complete comparison context.
- Do not add scrollytelling, sliders, or animation without a new explanatory task.
- Protect the evidence/mechanism distinction.
- Allow a Clear verdict.
