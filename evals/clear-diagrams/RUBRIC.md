# Clear Diagrams Eval Rubric

Score 0–3: question framing, topology/representation, path traceability, edge semantics, boundary/truth integrity, hierarchy, accessibility, and restraint.

Fail regardless of total for implying causality without support, presenting proposed systems as current, inventing unprovided components/events as observed fact, materially ambiguous arrows, deleting decision-critical exceptions, or replacing a strong simple diagram for novelty.
