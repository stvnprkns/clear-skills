# Case: org chart used for a workflow

**Family:** Rethink

## Prompt

Create an org chart showing how a customer refund moves from support to finance to payment operations, including rejection and retry paths.

## Expected skill behavior

- Identify sequence/branching as the topology, not organizational hierarchy.
- Recommend a flow or swimlane view with ownership boundaries.
- Preserve teams as lanes or labels rather than parent-child nodes.
- Include rejection/retry paths without showing every internal role.
