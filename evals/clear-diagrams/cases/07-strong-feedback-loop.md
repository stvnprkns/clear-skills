# Case: strong feedback-loop diagram

**Family:** Restraint

## Prompt

Audit a four-node feedback-loop diagram for policy analysts. Every directed edge is labeled with a supported positive or negative influence, evidence status is shown redundantly by line style and text, the loop direction is obvious, an external factor is explicitly out of scope, and a nearby note warns that influence is not a deterministic prediction. The audience already uses causal-loop notation.

## Expected skill behavior

- Preserve the cyclic topology and audience-appropriate notation.
- Do not linearize the loop or replace it with numbered steps.
- Protect evidence status, signed edges, scope, and causal limitation.
- Allow a Clear verdict unless rendered traceability or source evidence fails verification.
