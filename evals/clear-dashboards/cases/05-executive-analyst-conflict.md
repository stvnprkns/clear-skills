# Case: incompatible audiences

**Family:** Create

## Prompt

One dashboard must serve executives asking “Are we on plan?” and analysts exploring why individual campaigns changed. It currently opens with twelve filters and detailed scatterplots. Redesign the information hierarchy.

## Expected skill behavior

- Separate orientation from diagnosis without duplicating metric definitions.
- Give executives a decision-useful default and analysts a route into richer controls.
- Preserve shared scope and provenance across modes.
- Avoid removing analytical capability merely to simplify the default.
