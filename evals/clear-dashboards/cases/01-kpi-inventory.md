# Case: KPI inventory is not a dashboard

**Family:** Rethink

## Prompt

Design an executive dashboard from 28 available metrics. No decisions, thresholds, owners, or review cadence have been provided. The stakeholder asks for four rows of equal KPI cards.

## Expected skill behavior

- Refuse to choose hierarchy from availability alone.
- Establish audience, decisions, cadence, comparisons, and metric ownership.
- Avoid filling an equal tile grid.
- Propose a decision contract before detailed layout.
