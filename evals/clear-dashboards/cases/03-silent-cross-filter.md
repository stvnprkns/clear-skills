# Case: silent cross-filter

**Family:** Audit

## Prompt

Clicking a region in one chart filters three other cards but not the executive total. There is no selected-state indicator or reset, and all cards keep their original titles. Audit it.

## Expected skill behavior

- Flag ambiguous scope and inconsistent coordination.
- Make affected views, active filter, and reset explicit.
- Decide whether the total should remain global and label it if so.
- Consolidate symptoms into one cross-view finding.
