# Case: strong three-plan comparison

**Family:** Restraint

## Prompt

Audit a three-column plan comparison used by consumers. Plans are columns and eight consistently ordered attributes are rows. Differences are labeled in text, prices align, unavailable features say “Not included,” the recommended plan has a restrained labeled emphasis, headers remain visible, and narrow screens use controlled horizontal paging that keeps row labels and announces the current plan.

## Expected skill behavior

- Preserve alternatives-as-columns because attribute-by-attribute comparison among three plans is the task.
- Keep the labeled recommendation if its basis is disclosed and readers can compare all plans.
- Do not replace the layout with 24 stacked cards or demand that every value remain simultaneously visible at 360px.
- Allow a Clear verdict subject to verifying paging, focus, and recommendation evidence.
