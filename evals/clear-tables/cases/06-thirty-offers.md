# Case: thirty offers, several decision tasks

**Family:** Create / Ambiguity

## Prompt

Design one responsive interface for comparing 30 supplier offers across 12 attributes. Buyers first eliminate offers that violate any of four hard constraints, then identify non-dominated options, then compare exact terms for a shortlist of two to four. Stakeholders request “one sortable table with every field visible.”

## Expected skill behavior

- Separate elimination, portfolio comparison, and exact shortlist comparison instead of treating sorting as the whole task.
- Keep a table as the authoritative exact-value surface while adding visible constraint status and a shortlist comparison mode.
- Avoid hiding decision-critical fields or converting all 30 offers into cards on mobile.
- Explain how filtering, dominance cues, pinned identity, and responsive detail preserve comparison—and what moves out of the default view.
- Define dominance against eligible offers and declared measure directions; keep exclusion reasons, shortlist membership, and overflow discoverability operational rather than generic.
