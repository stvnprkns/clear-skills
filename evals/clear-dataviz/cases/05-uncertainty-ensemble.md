# Case: uncertainty ensemble

**Family:** Create

## Prompt

Design a view for flood planners choosing whether to close a road. Five hundred simulation paths cross the road threshold at different times; probability and timing both change the decision. A mean line hides the tail risk.

## Expected skill behavior

- Establish a standard interval/quantile baseline.
- Consider ensemble, quantile dot, or probability-over-time forms tied to the closure threshold.
- Explain simulation assumptions and avoid an unreadable 500-line overlay.
- Provide decision-relevant probability/timing and accessible summary.
