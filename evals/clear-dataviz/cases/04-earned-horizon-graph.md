# Case: earned horizon graph

**Family:** Restraint

## Prompt

Audit a horizon graph used by trained grid operators to monitor 60 sensors in limited vertical space. Bands, sign, and scale are documented; operators have training; exact values are available on keyboard focus and in a table; alarms do not rely on color alone.

## Expected skill behavior

- Preserve the specialized form because density and trained pattern scanning justify it.
- Do not replace it with 60 full-height line charts automatically.
- Protect non-color alarms and exact-value fallback.
- Verify representative and edge states rather than rejecting unfamiliarity.
